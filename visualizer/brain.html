Method and runtime

Each full stimulus is fitted proportionally inside a centered 256 × 256 area on a 292 × 292 padded canvas. The encoder removes the outer border. Maps use the first predicted response, with equal weight per stimulus.

These are model predictions, not measured fMRI, percent BOLD, or evidence of a causal feature effect. Feature groups can differ on other attributes. Small groups may be unstable.
