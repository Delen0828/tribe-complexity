Evaluation protocol

Each chart contributes three simulated TRIBE v2 responses at 20,484 fsaverage5 vertices. Five outer folds group images by source SHA-256. Different files can share a template. Each fit standardizes features using its training data and selects Ridge α by mean validation MAE across five grouped inner folds. R² pools the held-out predictions across all five outer folds.
